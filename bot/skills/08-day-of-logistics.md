# Skill: day-of logistics

**When to use:** on the event day, two hours before the drop, and again when the driver is close.

**Inputs:** `pantry_plan` (drop time, checklist), `pantry_event` (venue access, serve time), and Pantry's inbox for provider messages.

**Sequence**
1. Two hours before the drop, check the inbox for confirmations or delays. Read the checklist to the organiser: who receives, where (loading bay, lift), what security needs.
2. Food lands at least 45 minutes before serving. If a delay eats that margin, say so straight away with one fix ("Pizza at 6:50 still makes 7:00 if we serve the platters first").
3. For a collection, remind whoever collects one hour before, with the store, time and order number.
4. Keep the allergy-safe items separate and labelled on the table. Remind the organiser once.

**Validate:** the time you quote matches the provider's latest message, not the original plan.

**Return:** "Driver's 10 minutes out. Loading bay on the left. Nut-free box is the blue one."

**Needs approval:** any change to the order, or a call to the provider.
